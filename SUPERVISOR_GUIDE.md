## 🧭 Application Walkthrough & Key Sections

1. **Header & Presentation Mode:**
   - Click the **[Presentation Mode]** button in the top navbar to enter a distraction-free fullscreen presentation interface designed for the live ACROSET 2026 stage presentation.

2. **Research Supervisor & Author Profiles (Sections 2–4):**
   - Contains formal academic biographies, verified emails, and institutional affiliations for Prof. Dr. Praveen Kumar Sharma, Slock Ahuja, Dr. C. D. Parmar, and Indu Jaiswal.

3. **Theoretical Model & AI Workflow (Sections 5–7):**
   - 7-step visual problem cascade and 8 core technical challenges in 6G NTN.
   - Interactive multi-tier architecture inspector (Space, Aerial HAPS/UAV, Terrestrial gNB, Ground Users, PPO Engine).
   - 8-stage Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) reinforcement learning decision pipeline.

4. **Live Interactive NTN-6G Simulation Dashboard (Section 8):**
   - **User Count Slider:** Scale from 5 to 500 concurrent ground terminals across Urban, Rural, Maritime, Remote, and Disaster zones.
   - **LEO Satellite Slider:** Adjust constellation density from 1 to 50 satellites.
   - **PPO AI Toggle (ON/OFF):** Observe how AI eliminates co-channel collisions, reduces ping-pong handovers, and optimizes transmit power.
   - **Interactive Node Inspector:** Click any satellite or user terminal in the canvas to inspect instantaneous SINR, channel gain, and handover state in real time.

5. **Empirical Benchmarks & Dataset (Sections 9–12):**
   - Verified paper comparison figures: **2587.56 Mbps** throughput (+14.82%), **3.0%** packet loss (-62.5%), and **30 ms** handover latency (-45.45%).
   - Dataset specification breakdown covering ~12,000 samples and 11 telemetry features.

6. **Official Citation & BibTeX (Section 14):**
   - IEEE proceedings citation with one-click BibTeX copy button.
